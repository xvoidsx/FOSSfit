# FOSSfit
*your open-source partner in health and fitness*
<img width="1366" height="676" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/1be2f865-fc85-4693-ae08-fdb84b6f8a7b" />

**FOSSfit** is a free and open-source application for managing your health and fitness.

It has some of the following features for building and tracking healthier habits:

- **My Health Journey**: Log your own personal health journey in your own words - what's your mindset as you work on those healthier habits?
- **Food Diary**: Track your daily meals so you can see how you're doing! Are you eating healthier, or do you notice yourself falling back into old habits?
- **Medication Tracker**: Log your medicines and the times you take them so you can stay on top of prescriptions and supplements.
- **Weight Tracker**: Keep a routine weight log so you can visualize your progress!
- **My Health Goals**: Got any specific health goals you want to hit? Log them here so you can remind yourself of them.

### roadmap

We aim to create a great open-source health and fitness app that you can trust. Your data always stays locally on your own devices, unless you choose to place it somewhere else. Your health data is sensitive and personal, and nobody but you should have access to it!

To that end, we are working on some more features past the initial **1.0** stable release. We are also aiming to build an Android version of the app, so that you can use it on the go. We aim to also build a "pipeline" of sorts, so that you can sync your data from the desktop version of FOSSfit to the mobile version. 

Other features that are planned for FOSSfit include:

- **Blood pressure logging**: Record your blood pressure (systolic/diastolic values) in order to expand tracking for your cardiovascular health - could be a huge help for people who deal with things like hypertension!
- **Exercise logging**: Log types of exercises you do and times you do them, so you can visualize your exercise journey over time.
- **Health statistics**: Display basic statistics and trends over time, providing insights into how you've been doing across all fields as your health journey goes on!
- **Meditation and mindfulness helper**: Play ambient sounds with a set timer to allow the user to meditate with the app. Users can select sounds such as rain, forest sounds, ocean waves, white noise, or other options to meditate along to while the timer runs.
- **Data export/import**: Allows you to export your health data from one FOSSfit instance on a device into another FOSSfit instance so you can keep track of your health data across your various devices, if you so choose!

We have a lot of ideas to iterate on, and we hope to bring these and other features in over time to make FOSSfit your #1 partner in your health and fitness journey.

Over the longer term, we aim to bring the mobile version to fruition, which will also allow you to sync up with open hardware such as the [PineTime](https://pine64.org/devices/pinetime/) or [Pebble](https://repebble.com/) to further track things such as step count, heart rate, and more!
